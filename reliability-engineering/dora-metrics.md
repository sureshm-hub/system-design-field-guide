# DORA Metrics

 - named after the DevOps Research and Assessment (DORA) group

## The Four Key DORA Metrics

* Deployment Frequency (DF): How often an organization successfully releases to production (e.g., daily, weekly).
* Lead Time for Changes (MLTC): The time it takes for a commit to get into production.
* Change Failure Rate (CFR): The percentage of deployments that fail and require immediate intervention (e.g., rollbacks, hotfixes).
* Time to Restore Service (MTTR): How long it takes to recover from a production failure.

## Performance Benchmarks
DORA categorizes teams into performance tiers

* Elite: Multiple deploys/day, <1 hour lead time, <15% failure rate, <1 hour recovery time.
* Low: Once per month/less, 1-6 months lead time, 16-30% failure rate, 1 week-1 month recovery time.

## Benefits and Use Cases
* Engineering Leadership: Quantifies team performance and ROI of initiatives.
* Improvement: Helps identify bottlenecks to improve speed and quality.
* Predictability: Enables understanding of team performance ranges and outliers.
