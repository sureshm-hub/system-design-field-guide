# TFS Applications in Insurance, Claims,  Warranty / VPP (Vehicle Protection Products)

At TFS, these three domains are not generic insurance-only silos. 
They sit around the vehicle ownership / protection lifecycle:

## Insurance
- Quote / bind / policy purchase
- Customer self-service portal + mobile flows
- Agent / call-center assisted sales
- Dealer-assisted insurance offers
- Telematics / driving-data discount platform
- Billing, renewals, endorsements, cancellations
- Partner / carrier integration layer
- **Who Uses it:** Toyota says Toyota Auto Insurance supports purchase through 
  mobile app, website, call center, and dealerships, and that TIMS/TIMS 
  affiliates support broader insurance offerings like auto, home, renters, RV, 
  and pet.

## Claims
- FNOL ("First Notice of Loss") / claim intake
- Claim tracking portal
- Dealer repair claim submission
- GAP total-loss claim workflow
- Claims document upload / verification
- Status notifications / customer communication
- Claims routing to underwriters / repair / lender workflows
- **Who Uses it:** TFS explicitly says dealers can submit repair and 
  maintenance claims, and GAP claims can be submitted and tracked digitally 
  through a self-service portal.

## Warranty / VPP (Vehicle Protection Products)
- Vehicle Service Agreement platform
- Coverage / eligibility engine
- Contract enrollment / administration
- Claims adjudication for covered repairs
- Prepaid maintenance administration
- Tire & wheel / key protection workflows
- Excess wear / GAP / ancillary protection product systems
- Dealer-facing admin and reimbursement systems
- **Who Uses it:** TFS publicly groups VSA, GAP, prepaid maintenance, tire & 
  wheel, and similar protection products under its vehicle protection 
  offerings, and its careers pages reference a dedicated Technical Lead – VPP 
  role in a regulated, enterprise-scale environment.
